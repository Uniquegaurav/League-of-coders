What you get

Live battles

2 to 20 players race the same set with a live scoreboard and chat.

Solo timed tests

Ratings and tiers

Multiplayer Elo. Beating stronger players gains more.

Sunday Cup

A weekly 60-minute public tournament for up to 20 players.

Keep your existing progress

You keep using your own LeetCode account. Battles read your accepted submissions, and you can skip problems you have already solved.

Questions

Do I lose my LeetCode history?

No. You keep using LeetCode as before; League of Coders only reads your recent accepted submissions to score battles.

Which problems are used?

A curated set of interview classics by default, or the full free LeetCode catalogue if the host chooses.
