Formats

1v1

Challenge any player from their profile. You get a private 30-minute room with one Easy and one Medium.

Group battle

Host a public or private lobby with your own mix, topics and duration.

Battle hour

A public battle every day at 9 PM IST. The lobby opens 30 minutes before and starts on its own.

Sunday Cup

60 minutes, one Easy, two Medium, one Hard. Winners are listed on the tournaments page.

Rules

  • Everyone gets the same shuffled problem set when the battle starts.
  • Solves count when LeetCode accepts your submission, using LeetCode’s own timestamp.
  • Points: 100 / 200 / 300 by difficulty, up to +50% for speed, +25 for the first solve of each problem.
  • Equal scores go to whoever solved more problems, then to the earlier last solve.
  • Rated battles move your Elo rating. Solo tests never do.

Questions

Which languages can I use?

Any language LeetCode supports, since you submit on LeetCode.

How do you stop cheating?

Accounts must prove LeetCode ownership, solve times come from LeetCode, and problems are hidden until the battle starts.
