How it works

  1. Link your LeetCode account once. You prove it is yours by adding a short code to your LeetCode profile ReadMe.
  2. Create a battle. Pick how many Easy, Medium and Hard problems, the topics and the time limit (10 to 180 minutes).
  3. Share the invite link. Up to 20 players can join. Everyone gets the same problem set when the host starts.
  4. Solve on LeetCode. Your accepted submissions are read from LeetCode and scored automatically. Nobody has to report their own result.

Why practising together works

You show up

A battle at 9 PM with three friends is harder to skip than a plan to "do two mediums tonight".

Real time pressure

Interviews are timed. Battles train the same skill: reading a problem fast and committing to an approach.

Honest scores

Points come from LeetCode's accepted-submission timestamps, so the scoreboard is the same for everyone.

Scoring

Each problem is worth 100 (Easy), 200 (Medium) or 300 (Hard) points. Solving early adds up to 50% as a speed bonus, and the first player to solve a problem gets 25 more. Rated battles (two or more players) move your Elo rating, starting from 1200.

No friends online right now?

Join the public battle hour every evening at 9 PM IST, or send a "beat my score" challenge: your friends get the same problems and clock and can play whenever they like.

Questions

Is it free?

No. Battles only use free LeetCode problems.

Where do I write code?

On LeetCode, as you normally would. League of Coders shows the problems and keeps score.
